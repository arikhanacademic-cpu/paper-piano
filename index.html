Paper Piano

Print a keyboard, point a camera at it, and play it with your fingers.

Your camera feed appears here. Nothing leaves your device.

Set up

  1. Print the keyboard at 100% and tape it to a desk.
  2. Point a camera down at it from above or in front.
  3. Click the red dots 1, 2, 3, 4 in the camera view.
Download keyboard

Models load when you start the camera.
